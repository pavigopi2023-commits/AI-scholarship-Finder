The Problem

  • ⚠️ Complex eligibility criteria and financial income limits can be confusing.

About This Project

AI Scholarship Finder is a college project demonstrating a Rule-Based Scholarship Eligibility & Matching Engine. It compares your profile (education level, marks, income, state, category) against sample scholarship requirements, scores the fit out of 100, and explains which criteria matched.
